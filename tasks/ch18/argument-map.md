# Chapter 18 argument map

## Claim

Distributed execution must still connect to an institution that can explain, stop, and repair consequential outcomes. This is an operating principle; liability and statutory duties are separately determined by law.

## Support

- Current EU AI Act deployer oversight requirements and timeline are cited narrowly.
- Traceability, recourse, and repair are proposed operational requirements.
- The hiring example is explicitly illustrative.

## Limits

- Accountability vocabulary differs across law and management practice.
- AI Act scope and obligations depend on classification, role, and use; this chapter is not legal advice or a full compliance manual.
- No claim is made that one named individual must bear all responsibility.
