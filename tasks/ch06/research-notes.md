# Chapter 6 research notes

Checked: 29 September 2026

## Sources

- **NIST AI 600-1, Generative AI Profile (26 July 2024):** identifies both aversion to and over-reliance on generative AI in human-AI configurations, and describes automation bias as excessive deference. Voluntary risk-management guidance; it does not validate the chapter's matrix.
- **OpenAI, A Practical Guide to Building Agents (2025):** first-party product/engineering guidance recommends defined intervention and approval paths for sensitive or irreversible actions. It does not demonstrate comparative effectiveness.
- **OpenAI, Designing AI Agents to Resist Prompt Injection (11 March 2026):** first-party security guidance on containing impact when attacks succeed. It does not prove that defenses eliminate risk.
- **Gans, The Microeconomics of Artificial Intelligence (2025):** conceptual economic framing for prediction and complementary decision inputs. Chapter uses it as a lens, not evidence for the authority matrix.

## Evidence boundary

No quantitative claim about approval failure, decision latency, or automation-bias prevalence is made. The four authority permissions and consequence/reversibility matrix are the author's proposed design tools. Refund, pricing, hiring, and capacity cases are illustrations, not observed deployments.
