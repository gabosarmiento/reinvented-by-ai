Reviewed: 29 September 2026

## Findings and dispositions

### High — matrix could be read as a validated autonomy rule

Explicitly labeled it as a starting posture, not a formula, and added aggregate exposure and affected-party considerations.

### High — “human in the loop” critique lacked an evidence boundary

Added NIST's current risk framing for automation bias, while saying the impact depends on task and review design. The approval interface is presented as a design proposal, not a guarantee.

### Medium — product guidance and prompt-injection claims needed source limits

Marked OpenAI recommendations as first-party guidance and stated that containment does not eliminate prompt injection.

### Medium — founder and escalation language was too categorical

Changed claims to conditional statements; kept the illustrative cases and specified what escalation data may reveal.
