# Chapter 6 audit notes

Reviewed: 29 September 2026

## Argument

Chapter 6 treats decision rights as a design question before automation. Its inventory includes owner, inputs, criteria, consequence, reversibility, time, authority, evidence, escalation, appeal, and review date. It proposes a consequence/reversibility matrix and separates access, recommendation, commitment, and modification authority.

## Editorial findings

- Recast the matrix as a starting posture, not an empirically validated formula.
- Clarified automation-bias risk with the 2024 NIST GenAI Profile; avoided claiming human review always causes over-reliance.
- Marked OpenAI guidance as first-party engineering advice and not comparative evidence for oversight effectiveness.
- Clarified that prompt-injection controls reduce or contain risk; they do not guarantee elimination.
- Reworded broad founder/approval-queue assertions conditionally and labeled the pricing scenario illustrative.

## Open items

- Decision-rights matrix diagram and worked case require final visual/technical QA.
- Because the suggested record export may include personal or commercial data, final wording should be checked against applicable internal and legal controls before publication.
