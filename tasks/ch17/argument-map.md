# Chapter 17 argument map

## Claim

If AI reduces some information-assembly work, managers may need to emphasize intent, decision rights, exception handling, context stewardship, and judgment development. This is a hypothesis for organizational design, not a forecast that management disappears.

## Support

- Recent randomized studies show particular changes in information work and team collaboration (S006, S035).
- Evidence does not establish structural or management-role effects; claim is explicitly bounded.
- The Synthesizer is proposed as a responsibility, not a mandatory role.

## Limits

- Studies are specific to office-assistant use and product-development tasks.
- No validated span-of-control recommendation.
- Managerial-week exercise is a proposed diagnostic.
