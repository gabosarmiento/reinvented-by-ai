# Chapter 17 research notes

Evidence checked 29 September 2026. The chapter's accountabilities and role proposals are not empirically validated organizational prescriptions.

## Coordination and collaboration

- Dillon et al. (2025 NBER revision), a randomized experiment across 66 firms and 7,137 workers, found changed email time among frequent users and no significant meeting-time or task-composition change from individual access alone (S006).
- Dell'Acqua et al. (2026), preregistered field experiment with 791 P&G professionals on product-innovation work, found AI-assisted individuals performed at the level of unaided teams and produced more balanced solutions across commercial/R&D perspectives (S035).
- Neither study tested management structures, reporting spans, or the proposed Synthesizer role. The manuscript treats any organizational consequences as a hypothesis.

## Managerial work

- No current evidence found for a universal span-of-control or headcount threshold; removed categorical 40-to-150 staffing assertion.
- Synthesizer is a proposed responsibility that can be held within existing roles, not a demonstrated new job family.
- Founder-delegation vignette is conditional and should be treated as a scenario unless replaced with author experience.
