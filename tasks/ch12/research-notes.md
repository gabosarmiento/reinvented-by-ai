# Chapter 12 research notes

Reviewed: 29 September 2026

The chapter is implementation guidance and contains no external measured claim. Claims about sandboxing, credentials, event logging, revocation, and production feasibility should be read as requirements/design goals, not guarantees. The 30-day sequence is illustrative and depends on integration, consequence, regulation, and company capacity. Related technical sources are covered in Chapters 5, 7, 9, and 10; re-check them for implementation-specific statements at publication.
