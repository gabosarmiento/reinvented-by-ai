# Chapter 9 argument map

## Proposition

Governance should be implemented in the path of work through scoped identity, external permission checks, evidence, intervention, and recourse.

## Support

1. Prompt instructions do not restrict credentials.
2. Protocol authentication and authorization are necessary but not sufficient deployment controls.
3. Safe failure, cumulative limits, and authority separation contain action-level impact.
4. Reviews need qualified decision rights and queue capacity.
5. Affected parties need a route to challenge and repair outcomes.
6. Legal obligations apply by use, role, and risk class; the current schedule is staged.

## Boundary

The control-plane functions are a proposed operating architecture, not certification of compliance or security. Vendor security guidance is first-party. Current law and protocols require publication-time review.
