# Chapter 16 argument map

## Claim

When multiple agents share identity, tools, data, and dependencies, the organization needs a visible way to assign ownership, authorization, evaluation, change, incident response, and retirement.

## Support

- NIST 2026 agent identity work and OWASP 2025 threat taxonomy identify relevant open design risks (S034).
- MCP authorization spec describes protocol controls; these do not secure a full deployment.
- Registry and Agent Operations are the author's proposed response, not validated universal organizational forms.

## Limits

- No measured evidence that a central Agent Operations group reduces incidents or cost.
- Threat taxonomy is not incidence data.
- Illustrative supplier incident must not be read as real.
