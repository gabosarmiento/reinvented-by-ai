# Chapter 10 argument map

## Proposition

To manage agentic work, retain evidence about the decision path and resulting business state, evaluate behavior before and during use, and give different owners authority over system changes.

## Support

1. Technical completion is not the same as a verified business outcome.
2. Traces and decision records support different questions.
3. Outcome, flow, agent, control, and learning signals should remain distinct.
4. Appropriate stopping and escalation can be successful behavior.
5. Overrides and incidents need reasons and outcomes to inform controlled change.

## Boundary

Observability measures do not establish causation. Vendor dashboards do not prove enterprise value. The chapter's architecture is proposed and needs contextual validation.
