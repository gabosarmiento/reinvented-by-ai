# Chapter 11 argument map

## Proposition

Choose a pilot around a recurring commitment with a measurable complete path, bounded consequence, and accountable owner.

## Support

1. Department/tool framing does not identify a decision or outcome.
2. Candidate criteria surface value, frequency, latency, context, reversibility, consequence, integration, ownership, and transfer.
3. Exclusions and kill conditions cap early exposure.
4. A one-page charter names the learning question, authority, evidence, and exit decision.

## Boundary

The table and learning-density concept organize judgment; they do not predict success. Illustrative candidates are not empirical cases.
