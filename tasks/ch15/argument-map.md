# Chapter 15 argument map

## Claim

Adaptability requires changing commitments when relevant evidence justifies it, at a cadence that accounts for reversibility and the people/capabilities displaced.

## Support

- Recent OECD employer survey documents both manager-perceived benefits and governance concerns in algorithmic management (S033); it is contextual, not causal validation.
- Resource cadence, staged funding, talent-loan terms, and maintained knowledge products are proposed practices.
- The internal matching scenario is illustrative.

## Limits

- No evidence here that AI allocation rankings improve returns or that internal marketplaces create fairer matching.
- Worker consent, privacy, labor requirements, and adverse distributional effects require local review.
- Strategic allocation remains accountable to human portfolio owners in the proposed model.
