## Disposition

### KEEP

- Hidden Operational Debt as an operating liability that recurs when designed work paths and actual work diverge.
- The six diagnostic categories: decision, context, coordination, integration, knowledge, and control.
- Operational traces, outcomes, and cross-functional pricing exceptions as the practical evidence base.
- The warning that operational measurement can become employee surveillance.

### EVOLVE

- Present the six categories as an author-developed diagnostic lens, not an empirically validated taxonomy.
- Define principal as the unresolved operating-design gap and interest as its recurring cost or exposure; keep both in natural units until monetary conversion is defensible.
- Replace claims of a general AI-enabled change in diagnostic economics with a narrower claim about faster first-pass classification, subject to source review.
- Use current evaluation and metric-definition examples to distinguish traces from outcomes and preserve vendor-source limitations.

### REMOVE OR REVISE

- Broad claims that a model can inspect workflows without substantial study or review.
- The phrase “confusion at machine speed.”
- Product naming in the reader prompt; use the organization's approved model.
- Any implication that the six categories are a settled research taxonomy.
