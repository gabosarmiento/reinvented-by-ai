# Chapter 13 argument map

## Proposition

Expand a pilot only when outcome, total cost, risk, human load, and operating ownership support the proposed increase in scope.

## Support

1. Compare complete paths and include hidden work and failure costs.
2. Use a credible comparison and state the strength of causal inference.
3. Segment outcomes and inspect tails and displaced work.
4. Separate decision, system, and outcome quality.
5. Choose explicitly among expand, revise, contain, and stop.
6. Require a normal owner and sustainable maintenance before transfer.

## Boundary

The framework does not provide a universal ROI formula or causal proof. It is a proposed executive review discipline that depends on local measures and constraints.
