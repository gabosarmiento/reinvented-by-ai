# Chapter 4 audit notes

Reviewed: 29 September 2026

## Argument

Chapter 4 introduces the proposed ATOM architecture: intent, context, decisions, execution, evidence, learning, and cross-cutting control. It defines boundaries, illustrates the loop with a refund scenario, and positions the model relative to existing operating approaches.

## Editorial findings

- Present ATOM consistently as this book's proposed synthesis, not an established or validated framework.
- Removed a duplicated definition of the Playbook and clarified who curates learning.
- Labeled the refund scenario illustrative and described the model as a proposal rather than an empirically validated universal template.
- The chapter is conceptual and contains no external quantitative claim requiring additional current data. Later chapters carry the standards, technical, legal, and implementation details and need their own current evidence checks.
- The claim that the model draws on familiar flow/feedback approaches is conceptual; avoid priority claims until comparisons in the outline are properly sourced.

## Open items

- The outline specifies a six-layer canonical diagram; it exists, but diagram/source and multi-format checks belong to final production.
- Final terminology, reference, link, and render checks remain open.
