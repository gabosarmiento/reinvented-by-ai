# Chapter 4 editorial review

Reviewed: 29 September 2026

## Findings

### High — proposed architecture sounded final and established

Changed the opening and contribution claims to say this book proposes a model. The chapter no longer says “complete model”; it says later chapters develop the proposal and where it may be useful.

### Medium — the Playbook was defined twice

Merged the two adjacent descriptions, preserving the requirement for an accountable owner to approve changes before production behavior changes.

### Medium — example and transfer claims needed clearer bounds

Marked the refund scenario illustrative and clarified that similar decisions need controls proportionate to consequence; the loop is not proof of a universal template.

### Medium — distinctiveness was asserted without comparison

Recast the difference from Lean, Agile, and product operating models as the book's proposed contribution, without claiming novelty. Detailed comparison remains for the later model-comparison work.

## Remaining release conditions

No final multi-format rendering, diagram verification, or terminology/link check has been run. No claim of publication readiness is made.
