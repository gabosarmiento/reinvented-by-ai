# Chapter 14 research notes

Evidence checked 29 September 2026. The core chapter is a proposed operating model; the cited literature is context, not model validation.

## Boundary management

- Leicht-Deobald et al. (2025 issue; online 2023), meta-analysis of 85 studies and 10,848 teams. Overall boundary management is positively associated with team performance; effects vary by boundary activity and target. Added as S032 and cited in the chapter.
- Limitation: teams and practices across decades; no test of Fractal Units or AI-enabled organizations. Do not claim causality or transfer the estimated association directly to this model.

## Organizational model comparison

- Existing team-topology and platform operating models overlap with the chapter's bounded teams, interfaces, and shared services. This chapter currently acknowledges overlap, but a substantive comparative treatment against named models remains an editorial release condition.
- Claims about reorganization, team continuity, and boundary placement remain conditional practical reasoning; no recent causal evidence was identified to support universal rules.

## Scenarios

- The Aster scaling case is fictional. Its prices, usage, customer counts, and unit boundaries are chosen assumptions, not observations or a forecast.
- Customer counts are not asserted as universal thresholds for organizational change.
