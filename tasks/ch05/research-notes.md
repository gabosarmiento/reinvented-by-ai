# Chapter 5 research notes

Checked: 29 September 2026

## Sources used

- **Anthropic, “Effective context engineering for AI agents” (29 September 2025):** first-party engineering guidance on context selection, finite context windows, tools, retrieval, memory, and long-horizon work. The chapter uses it only for context as an engineering resource and explicitly distinguishes it from independent organizational evidence.
- **NIST AI 600-1, Generative AI Profile (26 July 2024):** voluntary risk-management profile with lifecycle practices involving governance, provenance, privacy, testing, and incidents. Within 24 months on the review date. It does not prescribe the chapter's envelope or act as a technical standard.
- **OpenAI, “Designing AI agents to resist prompt injection” (11 March 2026):** first-party security guidance describing prompt injection as evolving social engineering and emphasizing constrained impact, source-sink analysis, and safeguards around consequential actions. It does not claim that any control eliminates injection.

## Boundary

No empirical claim that context envelopes improve business outcomes is made. The four tests, memory categories, and context-product measures are the book's proposed operating practices. Supplier, pricing, and refund cases are illustrative.
