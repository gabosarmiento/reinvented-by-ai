# Decision 0006: Treat the Book as the First Public Edition and Focus on the Founder-COO

Date: 12 September 2026.

Status: accepted after author review of Chapter 1.

## Context

The source PDFs were abandoned working drafts. They were never published. Presenting the current book as a revision of a public predecessor would create a false publication history and burden the reader with an irrelevant origin story.

The initial audience definition also aimed too high and too broadly. The author supplied a more useful reader situation: a founder-COO taking a company from about 40 people toward 150 or 200, moving from one activity to several, handing the run to an operating leader, and struggling to delegate decisions rather than tasks.

## Decision

The manuscript and public positioning will treat *The Adaptive Company* as the first public book. They will not mention an earlier edition, an abandoned draft, or a retrospective change-of-mind narrative.

The editorial audit may retain internal source provenance because it documents how claims were assessed. That provenance will not enter the published manuscript, cover copy, preface, or author narrative.

The primary reader is now the founder-COO or equivalent operator facing the 40-to-200-person transition. Enterprise readers remain secondary. The central leadership problem is that the founder has delegated execution while retaining integration, arbitration, and decision authority.

## Consequences

- The preface becomes “The Company Is the Design Problem,” not a history of the manuscript.
- Chapter 1 opens with a labeled founder-COO composite rather than a product-team vignette.
- The chapter test asks whether the material helps the company decide without routing every consequential choice through the founder.
- Business-unit design, shared functions, initiative-to-run transfer, unit economics, middle management, and peer influence must appear across the book where they change the operating-model argument.
- The working title and eighteen-chapter structure remain intact for now.
