# Decision 0004 — Delay ATOM Until the Problem Is Earned

Date: 11 September 2026  
Status: accepted for Chapter 1

## Context

Both original PDFs introduce ATOM immediately after an urgency argument. The move makes the model read as a marketed answer rather than a reasoned response. The approved outline places the canonical definition in Chapter 4, but Chapter 1 still needed a decision about whether to preview the name.

## Decision

Chapter 1 will establish the moving-constraint problem without naming ATOM as the solution. It will end with Hidden Operational Debt, the subject of Chapter 2. ATOM will first be defined after the reader has seen the constraint, the debt it creates, and the limitations of tool-level adoption.

The chapter adds a five-queue diagnostic—selection, context, integration, verification, and authorization—with accountability surrounding the flow. This is not a replacement for the six-layer ATOM architecture. It is a diagnostic view of where cheap production creates pressure.

## Consequences

- The book takes three chapters to earn the operating-model proposal.
- The opening is less promotional and more falsifiable.
- Later chapters must keep the queue diagnostic distinct from ATOM’s canonical layers.
- No change to the approved eighteen-chapter structure is required.
