# Changelog

This file records intellectual and structural changes during development of the book. It is not a sentence-level change log and does not form part of the reader-facing narrative.

## Unreleased

### Chapter 1 — When Production Gets Cheap

- Replaced the generic product-team opening with a labeled founder-COO composite: a company moving from about 40 people toward 150, adding a business line, and trying to move the COO from the run to organization design.
- Made the gap between delegated execution and retained decision authority the chapter’s leadership problem.
- Added Joshua Gans’s microeconomic distinction between cheaper prediction and the complete decision, then separated that source-backed frame from ATOM’s extension to tool-using agents, bounded authority, evidence, and recovery.
- Replaced invented “AI time” ratios and inevitability rhetoric with evidence on fixed-capability inference prices, bounded agent tasks, adoption, and heterogeneous productivity.
- Changed the opening argument from “AI moves faster than legacy companies” to “cheap production moves the operating constraint.”
- Introduced the distinction among output, throughput, outcome, and value.
- Added the five-queue diagnostic: selection, context, integration, verification, and authorization, with accountability surrounding the flow.
- Added explicit boundary conditions and a counterargument about agents absorbing downstream queues.
- Added a five-step Monday decision-delegation test and connected unresolved queues to Hidden Operational Debt.
- Deferred the definition of ATOM until Chapter 4 so the model is earned rather than announced.
- Replaced generic AI-wave illustrations with a version-controlled Mermaid diagram.
